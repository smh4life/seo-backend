# How to Find All Your Services in Render

## Finding All Services:

1. **You're already on render.com** - good!
2. **Look at the left sidebar** - you should see:
   - "Dashboard" (you're probably here)
   - "Services" - click this!
3. **In the Services list**, you should see all your services
   - You already found: "SEO – backend" (or "seo-backend")
   - Look for another one - might be called:
     - "SEO – frontend"
     - "seo-frontend"
     - "myseogenerator-frontend"
     - Or something similar

**Tell me: Do you see another service besides "SEO – backend"?**

---

## If You Only See One Service:

That's okay! It might mean:
- Your frontend and backend are in the same service (unlikely but possible)
- Or your frontend is hosted somewhere else
- Or we need to create a frontend service

**Either way, we'll figure it out!**
